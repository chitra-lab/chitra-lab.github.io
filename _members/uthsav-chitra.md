Uthsav is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Johns Hopkins University. 
He was previously a postdoctoral fellow in the [Eric and Wendy Schmidt Center](https://www.ericandwendyschmidtcenter.org/) 
at the [Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard](https://www.broadinstitute.org/). 
He received his PhD in Computer Science from [Princeton University](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/) advised by [Ben Raphael](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/~braphael/).

His research focuses on developing computational methods for addressing fundamental problems in biology. He is particularly excited about developing spatiotemporal and graph-based AI/ML algorithms for new biological technologies including single-cell/spatial-omics and CRISPR.
