James is a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Whiting School of Engineering at Johns Hopkins University. His primary research interests lie at the intersection of trajectory inference and spatial transcriptomics. James earned his PhD in Mathematics at the University of Muenster and has a research background in stochastic processes, information theory, and functional analysis. He can be reached at joquinn4@jh.edu.
